
## Blue Innovation — Premium Corporate Consulting Website

A world-class, top-tier consulting firm website built from scratch, inspired by McKinsey, BCG, and Deloitte Digital. Here's the full plan:

---

### 🎨 Design System
- **Color palette:** Deep corporate blue (#0A2463 primary), lighter blue accents (#1E5EFF), white & near-black text, very light gray backgrounds
- **Typography:** Inter font (via Google Fonts) — bold headlines, clean body text, strong hierarchy
- **Style:** Premium, minimal, generous whitespace, elegant spacing, no startup vibes

---

### 🧭 Header / Navbar
- Transparent on hero, transforms to white/solid with shadow on scroll
- Logo "Blue Innovation" on the left (text-based, bold)
- Navigation links: Home · Chi siamo · Servizi · Metodo · Contatti
- Right side: "Contattaci" primary CTA button
- Fully sticky, smooth transition behavior

---

### 🖼️ Section 1 — Hero (Full Screen)
- 100vh, corporate blue gradient background
- Fade-in animation on text and CTA on load
- Large bold headline: *"Costruiamo insieme il migliore percorso di innovazione per la tua azienda"*
- Subheadline: *"Supportiamo le imprese nel trasformare innovazione e strategia in risultati concreti."*
- Primary CTA: "Scopri come possiamo aiutarti" + Secondary: "Contattaci"
- Subtle decorative geometric/abstract element for premium feel

---

### 👥 Section 2 — Chi Siamo
- Two-column layout: rich text on left, abstract corporate graphic on right
- Communicates consulting expertise, strategic partnership, and innovation leadership
- Clean typography, generous padding

---

### 🛠️ Section 3 — Servizi
- 6 service cards in a responsive grid (2–3 cols)
- Services: Innovazione Strategica, Digital Transformation, Consulenza Aziendale, Supporto alla Crescita, Strategia & Sviluppo, Change Management
- Each card: icon + title + short description + hover lift/shadow animation

---

### 📐 Section 4 — Metodo
- Horizontal step timeline layout (desktop), vertical on mobile
- 4 steps: Analisi → Strategia → Implementazione → Crescita
- Numbered, connected with a line, clean minimal design

---

### 💡 Section 5 — Value Proposition
- 4-column grid of pillars
- Approccio Strategico · Orientamento ai Risultati · Supporto Continuo · Esperienza & Competenza
- Icons + title + 1–2 line description each

---

### 📣 Section 6 — Final CTA
- Full-width, blue gradient background
- Large text: *"Trasforma oggi il futuro della tua azienda."*
- Single prominent "Contattaci" button

---

### 🔻 Section 7 — Footer
- Dark minimal footer
- Logo, nav links, contact email, company info, copyright

---

### ✨ Animations
- Fade-in on scroll (Intersection Observer)
- Smooth hover effects on cards, buttons, nav links
- Scroll-based navbar transformation
- All animations subtle and premium

---

### 📱 Responsiveness
- Fully responsive: desktop, tablet, mobile
- Mobile-first layout with collapsed nav (hamburger menu)
- All sections reflow cleanly on small screens
